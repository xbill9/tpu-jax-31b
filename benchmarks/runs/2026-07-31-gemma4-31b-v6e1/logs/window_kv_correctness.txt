Random-token prompts, greedy, w4a16, bf16 cache. prefill_with_kv_cache only (NO chunking).

  S      sliding buf F/T     max|diff|   rel      argmax match

  At S=1024 the two buffers differ by EIGHT slots and both take the same

(2) Ground truth = same model, kv_caches=None (no cache at all), S=1024:
  no-cache            top1 = 237221
  window_kv=True      top1 = 237221   MATCH      max|diff| 7.29

CONCLUSION: prefill logits depend on the KV buffer's size, which they must not —
the docstring states prefill attends over the freshly computed K/V, not the cache.
window_kv=True agrees with the cache-free reference on argmax; window_kv=False
does not. Chunked prefill REQUIRES window_kv=False.

NOT ESTABLISHED: the mechanism. Reading the attention cache branch, prefill does
not reassign k/v (it only builds kv_out_override via _ring_store), so buffer size
should be unobservable. The large max|diff| against no-cache in BOTH modes is also
unexplained. Do not act on a guessed cause.
